Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2025

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and your choices. If you have any questions, contact us through the app store listing or our GitHub repository.

What we collect

  • Nothing. The app operates entirely offline. No personal information, analytics, or user data is collected, stored, or transmitted.

Data processing

  • All CSV files you import are processed locally on your device.
  • No data is sent to external servers, third parties, or cloud services.
  • The app does not require an internet connection to function.

Data storage

  • No data is permanently stored by the app. All imported CSV files and processed information remain only in your device's temporary memory during app usage.
  • When you close the app, all imported data is cleared from memory.

What we do not collect

  • No location data, contacts, photos, or other personal files.
  • No payment information.
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics or performance metrics.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

User accounts

The app does not require user accounts or any form of registration.

Children's privacy

Zoom Attendance Tracker is intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Since we don't collect or transmit any data, there's no risk of data breaches. All processing happens locally on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. We'll update the effective date above and, when appropriate, notify users through app updates.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app store listing or our GitHub repository.
